Frequently Asked Questions

Everything to know about our developing travel platform.

Can I book a trip on SHINYURIGAOK?

Not yet. This is a demonstration website and does not offer real reservations or travel purchases.

Are the prices on the website real?

No. Prices are illustrative starting estimates in USD for design purposes only, not actual quotes or offers.

Does the contact form send an email?

No. It is a local interface demonstration and does not send or save your inquiry.

Which destinations are featured?

Our inspiration catalog includes Paris, Santorini, Tokyo, the Maldives, the Swiss Alps, Dubai, Rome, Bali, and New York.

When will real booking become available?

No launch date has been announced. Any future booking service would require verified travel partners and secure integrations.
